ADMISSION PROCESS:

QUOTA SYSTEM:
- Admission through Government quota via TNEA counselling
- Management quota seats are also available


ADMISSION CONFIRMATION:
- Selected students receive confirmation via SMS or Email


FEES STRUCTURE:
- Only government-approved fees are collected
- No capitation fee is charged


REQUIRED DOCUMENTS:
- Mark sheets
- Transfer Certificate (TC)
- Community Certificate
- Income Certificate
- First Graduate Certificate (if applicable)
- Passport size photographs


ELIGIBILITY FOR FIRST YEAR ADMISSION:

- OC category: Minimum 45% in Maths, Physics, Chemistry
- BC, MBC, DNC, SC, ST: Minimum 40% in Maths, Physics, Chemistry


LATERAL ENTRY ADMISSION:

- Eligible: Diploma or B.Sc Mathematics students

- OC category: Minimum 45% marks
- BC, MBC, DNC, SC, ST: Minimum 40% marks
