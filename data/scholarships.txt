SCHOLARSHIPS AND FINANCIAL SUPPORT:

OVERVIEW:
- The college provides various scholarships to support students financially


POST-MATRIC SCHOLARSHIP:

- Available for BC, MBC, DNC, SC, and ST students
- Annual family income must be less than ₹2.5 lakhs


PRAGATI SCHEME:

- Available for girl students
- Family income must be less than ₹8 lakhs
- Provides ₹50,000 per year


SAKSHAM SCHEME:

- Available for specially-abled students
- Family income must be less than ₹8 lakhs
- Provides ₹50,000 per year


7.5% GOVERNMENT SCHOOL QUOTA:

- Full fee support for students from government schools


PUDHUMAI PENN SCHEME:

- Provides ₹1,000 per month for girl students


SINGLE PARENT SUPPORT:

- ₹5,000 reduction in annual fees
