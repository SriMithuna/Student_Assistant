PLACEMENT PROCESS AND GUIDELINES:

PLACEMENT PROCESS:

- Students must register for placement
- Pre-placement talks are conducted by companies
- Skill training sessions are provided
- Students apply for job opportunities
- Selection process includes:
  - Aptitude Test
  - Group Discussion
  - Technical/HR Interview
- Selected students receive offer letters
- Final step is onboarding into the company


ELIGIBILITY:

- All students are eligible to participate in placements


PLACEMENT RULES:

- Attendance for pre-placement talks is mandatory
- Students should not directly contact recruiters


DISCIPLINE RULES:

- Students who skip interviews after clearing tests will be debarred
