COLLEGE RULES AND REGULATIONS:

DRESS CODE:
- Students must wear college uniform
- Black shoes are mandatory


LANGUAGE POLICY:
- English must be spoken inside the campus


BIKE / TRANSPORT RULES:
- Students must submit driving license copy
- Triple riding is strictly prohibited


PROHIBITED ACTIVITIES:
- Mobile phones are not allowed inside campus
- Tobacco, alcohol, and drugs are strictly banned
- Ragging is strictly prohibited


CAMPUS RESTRICTIONS:
- Birthday celebrations are not allowed on campus
- Sitting on railings is not allowed
